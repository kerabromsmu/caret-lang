## Ordinary callable construction

The [ordinary-callable invariant](../spec/03-functions-operators-and-lambdas.md#function-application)
applies to specialized constructors as their value kinds are implemented. Existing `contract` and
`template` evidence does not establish support for the remaining facilities. These rows require
future runtime and runnable integration evidence, including aliases, shadowing, arity, partials,
dispatch, effects, reflection, and staging when available.
